# Question Frameworks for Progressive Discovery

This reference provides detailed question templates for each "dig deeper" topic in the PRD creation workflow.

## Target Audience Deep Dive

When user opts to dig deeper on target audience:

### Framework Questions:
1. **Demographic Profile**
   - Age range?
   - Geographic location?
   - Education level?
   - Income level (if relevant)?

2. **Psychographic Profile**
   - What are their values and beliefs?
   - What motivates them?
   - What are their pain points and frustrations?
   - What are their goals and aspirations?

3. **Behavioral Profile**
   - How do they currently solve this problem?
   - What tools/products do they already use?
   - Where do they spend their time online?
   - How tech-savvy are they?

4. **Segmentation**
   - Are there distinct user segments?
   - Which segment should we prioritize first?
   - What makes each segment unique?

### Example Application:

**User says**: "This is for small business owners"

**Dig Deeper Questions**:
1. "What industry are these small business owners in? (e.g., retail, services, tech)"
2. "How many employees do they typically have? (solo, 1-10, 10-50?)"
3. "Are they tech-savvy or do they struggle with digital tools?"
4. "What's their biggest challenge right now that your product addresses?"
5. "Do they have a budget for tools like this, or are they cost-sensitive?"

## Problem & Value Proposition Deep Dive

### Framework Questions:
1. **Problem Validation**
   - How do you know this problem exists?
   - How many people have this problem?
   - How painful is this problem? (nice-to-solve vs must-solve)
   - What happens if they don't solve it?

2. **Current Solutions**
   - How are people solving this today?
   - What's wrong with existing solutions?
   - Why isn't anyone else solving it this way?
   - What makes your approach better/different?

3. **Value Proposition**
   - What's the core benefit users get?
   - Why is this 10x better than alternatives?
   - What's the "aha moment" when users understand the value?

### Example Application:

**User says**: "This helps freelancers manage their invoices"

**Dig Deeper Questions**:
1. "What specifically is broken about how freelancers manage invoices today?"
2. "Are they using spreadsheets, other tools, or pen and paper?"
3. "What's the biggest pain point? (tracking payments, creating invoices, tax prep?)"
4. "How much time does this currently take them per month?"
5. "What's the cost of not solving this? (late payments, missed invoices, tax issues?)"

## Business Model Deep Dive

### Framework Questions:
1. **Revenue Model**
   - Who pays? (users, businesses, sponsors, ads)
   - How much? (free, freemium, subscription, one-time)
   - When do they pay? (upfront, after trial, usage-based)

2. **Pricing Strategy**
   - What's the value metric? (per user, per feature, per transaction)
   - What price range feels right for your target audience?
   - What do competitors charge?
   - Would users pay monthly or annually?

3. **Alternative Funding**
   - Sponsors or partnerships?
   - Grants or government funding?
   - Crowdfunding or pre-sales?
   - Affiliate or referral revenue?

### Example Application:

**User says**: "I'm not sure, maybe free to start"

**Dig Deeper Questions**:
1. "Do you eventually want to charge for this, or keep it free forever?"
2. "If you charge, would it be a subscription ($X/month) or one-time payment?"
3. "What's one feature you could charge for while keeping basic features free?"
4. "Would your target audience pay $10/month? $50/month? What feels right?"
5. "Are there any sponsors or partners who might fund this?"

## Key Features Deep Dive

### Framework Questions:
1. **Feature Prioritization**
   - What's the absolute minimum for v1?
   - Which features are must-have vs nice-to-have?
   - What can wait for v2?

2. **User Journey**
   - What's the first thing users do when they open the product?
   - Walk through a typical user session step-by-step
   - What's the core loop? (what do users do repeatedly)
   - What's the end goal users are trying to reach?

3. **Feature Details**
   - For each must-have feature:
     - What inputs does it need?
     - What outputs does it produce?
     - What happens if it fails or has errors?

### Example Application:

**User says**: "Users can create projects and track tasks"

**Dig Deeper Questions**:
1. "Let's start with creating a project. What information do users need to provide?"
2. "What happens after they create a project? Do they immediately add tasks?"
3. "For task tracking, what info does each task have? (due date, assignee, priority?)"
4. "Can users see all tasks in a list, or organized by project, or both?"
5. "What's the end goal? Marking tasks complete? Generating reports?"

## Success Metrics Deep Dive

### Framework Questions:
1. **Leading Indicators** (early signals)
   - What would show traction in week 1?
   - What would show traction in month 1?
   - What's the fastest feedback loop?

2. **Lagging Indicators** (long-term outcomes)
   - What does success look like in 6 months?
   - What does success look like in 1 year?
   - What's your "north star" metric?

3. **Failure Signals**
   - What would indicate this isn't working?
   - At what point would you pivot?
   - What's the minimum viable success?

### Example Application:

**User says**: "I want lots of users"

**Dig Deeper Questions**:
1. "How many signups would make you feel like this is working? (50? 500? 5000?)"
2. "More important: lots of signups, or fewer signups but highly engaged?"
3. "What's one action users take that shows they're getting value?"
4. "In 3 months, what specific number would you celebrate? (X active users, Y paying customers?)"
5. "What would make you say 'this isn't working, we should pivot'?"

## Long-term Vision Deep Dive

### Framework Questions:
1. **Scaling Challenges**
   - What happens when you have 10x users?
   - What will break first?
   - What infrastructure/team do you need?

2. **Product Roadmap**
   - What features come after v1?
   - What integrations do you want?
   - What would make this a "platform"?

3. **Business Evolution**
   - Do you want to bootstrap or raise funding?
   - Build a team or stay solo?
   - Sell the company or run it long-term?

### Example Application:

**User says**: "I see this becoming a big platform"

**Dig Deeper Questions**:
1. "What does 'big platform' mean to you? (millions of users? enterprise customers?)"
2. "What integrations would make this platform-like? (API, webhooks, plugins?)"
3. "In 12 months, what's the one feature that would make you most proud?"
4. "Do you plan to raise funding, or bootstrap this?"
5. "What's your exit strategy? (build to sell, build to run forever, build to IPO?)"

---

## Usage Tips

1. **Don't ask all questions** - Pick 3-5 most relevant for the user's context
2. **Follow the conversation** - Let user answers guide which follow-ups to ask
3. **Connect to product** - Always tie answers back to product decisions
4. **Offer examples** - Give specific options to make abstract questions concrete
5. **Allow "skip"** - Users can always say "not sure yet, let's move on"
